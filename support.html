Support

Hidden Ranks, by playbyear.

Hidden Ranks is a strategy game for one or two players on a 10 by 10 battlefield. Each side commands 40 pieces whose ranks stay hidden from the enemy until they fight. Capture the enemy Flag to win. It works fully with VoiceOver and other screen readers.

Get help or send feedback

Use the contact form for bug reports, questions and ideas, and say that it's about Hidden Ranks. You don't need an account. Add your email address if you'd like a reply. For a bug, it helps to say what you were doing and whether you use VoiceOver.

Common questions

How do I play?

The full rules are in the game, under How to play. In short: arrange your army in rows 1 to 4, start the battle, and move one piece a turn. Move onto an enemy piece to attack it. Both ranks are revealed and the stronger piece wins.

How do I move a piece on a touch screen?

Tap one of your pieces, or double-tap it with VoiceOver, to select it. The game reads where it can go. Then tap the square to move to. You can also type a move such as e4 e5 in the Commands box.

Does it need the internet?

No. Games against the computer work offline. Only playing a friend online needs a connection.

How do I play a friend?

Press Host an online game. You get a four-letter code and an invite link to send to your friend. They type the code under Online play, or open the link. A friend on the website and a friend in the app can play each other.

I closed the game in the middle of an online match.

Open the game again and press Rejoin game, or open the invite link again. The match picks up where it left off.

Are my saved setups kept?

Yes, on your device. In the iPhone app they're also kept as a file in the app's own storage, which iOS doesn't clear. Deleting the app deletes them.

Does the computer cheat?

No. It only knows what you would know: which of your pieces have moved, and which have been revealed in a fight.

Does it have ads, purchases or tracking?

No ads, nothing to buy, no accounts and no tracking. See the privacy policy.
