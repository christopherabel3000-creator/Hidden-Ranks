    How to play

    Rules, keys and commands

    Goal

    Capture the enemy Flag, or leave the enemy with no piece that can move. You can see your own ranks; enemy ranks stay hidden until a fight reveals them.

    Pieces, strongest first

    Marshal 10 (1), General 9 (1), Colonel 8 (2), Major 7 (3), Captain 6 (4), Lieutenant 5 (4), Sergeant 4 (4), Miner 3 (5), Scout 2 (8), Spy 1 (1), Bomb (6), Flag (1).

    Setting up

    • Your army starts in a random layout. Swap two pieces by selecting one and then the other, or move a piece by selecting it and then an empty square.
    • Press Place pieces myself to empty your rows and place all 40 pieces by hand. Choose a piece from your reserve, then press Enter on an empty square in rows 1 to 4. Place the rest randomly fills whatever is left.
    • On the board, press 1 to 9 to choose a piece, 0 for the Marshal, B for a Bomb or F for the Flag. Delete picks a placed piece back up.
    • Typed commands: place starts placing by hand, place captain e4 or place 6 e4 places a piece, remove e4 picks one up, reserve lists what's left, and fill places the rest randomly.

    Moving

    • Move one piece one square up, down, left or right each turn. No diagonals, and never into the lakes at C5, D5, G5, H5, C6, D6, G6 and H6.
    • Scouts move any distance in a straight line and can attack at the end. A Scout that moves more than one square reveals that it's a Scout.
    • Bombs and the Flag never move.
    • A piece can't go back and forth between the same two squares more than three moves in a row.

    Fighting

    • Move onto an enemy to attack. Both ranks are revealed and the higher rank wins. Equal ranks remove both.
    • The Spy defeats the Marshal, but only when the Spy attacks.
    • A Bomb destroys any attacker except a Miner, which defuses it.

    Keyboard on the battlefield

    • Arrow keys move between squares. Up moves toward the enemy side. Home and End jump to columns A and J.
    • Enter or Space selects your piece and reads where it can go. Press it again on a destination to move.
    • Escape cancels a selection. R repeats the last event.

    Typed commands

    • e4 e5 moves or attacks. During setup it swaps two of your pieces.
    • f7 describes one square. row 7 or column e reads a whole line.
    • moves lists every legal move; moves e4 lists one piece's moves.
    • known lists revealed enemy pieces. status counts what's left.
    • shuffle and start work during setup.

    Online play

    Press Host an online game to get a four-letter code and an invite link for your friend. You both arrange your armies, press I'm ready, and the host moves first. Each of you sees your own army in rows 1 to 4. If you lose your connection or close the page, open the invite link again or use Rejoin game to pick up where you left off.

    About the computer

    The computer plays fair. It only knows what you would know: which of your pieces have moved and which have been revealed in battle.
